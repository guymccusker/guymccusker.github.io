* Example: Silent vs Interaction
Apply both ways:

#+ATTR_REVEAL: :frag (appear)
- Black application:
#+BEGIN_SRC latex
I^\bullet \bullet D^\circ \;\to_\beta^\tau\; D^\circ
#+END_SRC

- White application:
#+BEGIN_SRC latex
I^\bullet \circ D^\circ \;\to_\beta\; D^\circ
#+END_SRC

---

* What Happened?
#+ATTR_REVEAL: :frag (appear)
- First case:
  - colours match (black / black)
  - ⇒ *silent step*

- Second case:
  - colours differ (black / white)
  - ⇒ *interaction step*

* Comparison Example
Compare:

#+BEGIN_SRC latex
I^\bullet
\qquad\text{vs}\qquad
(I^\bullet \circ I^\circ)
#+END_SRC

#+ATTR_REVEAL: :frag (appear)
- Both reduce to the same result

- But:
  - $I^\bullet$:
    - 0 interaction steps

  - $I^\bullet \circ I^\circ$:
    - 1 interaction step

---

* Interpretation
#+ATTR_REVEAL: :frag (appear)
- Same *extensional behaviour*

- Different *interaction cost*
